### Full Description (ASO-Optimized)
```text
Never miss a dose or wash out an eye drop again. MediMind Pro is the world’s first clinical-grade medication management and ophthalmic safety system, engineered to protect your vision, manage complex drug regimens, and keep you safe.

Unlike generic reminder apps that only tell you "take your medicine", MediMind Pro understands clinical pharmacology, ophthalmic sequencing, and drug interactions—running 100% offline on your device for complete medical privacy.

👁️ CLINICAL EYE DROP & OPHTHALMIC TIMING ASSISTANT
• Prevent Corneal Wash-Out: Your eye reservoir can only hold one drop. Instilling drops too close together washes out the active medicine. MediMind enforces a configurable 5-to-10 minute wait interval between consecutive drops.
• Drops vs. Ointments Sequencing: The app automatically ensures liquid eye drops are taken FIRST and oily eye ointments LAST, ensuring full absorption.
• Punctal Occlusion Guide: Guided 60-second nasolacrimal occlusion timer prevents drops from draining into systemic circulation, protecting your heart and blood pressure.
• 28-Day Bottle Sterility Window: Tracks the exact date eye drop bottles are opened and alerts you before microbial contamination risk occurs.
• Contact Lens Safety: Reminds you to remove contact lenses before drop administration and wait 15 minutes before re-inserting.

📷 AI PRESCRIPTION SCANNER & TYPO AUTO-CORRECTION
• Multi-Medication OCR: Snap a photo of physical doctor prescriptions or hospital discharge summaries to instantly extract all medications.
• Smart Notation Parsing: Accurately decodes Indian and international clinical matrix formats (1-0-1, 1-1-1, 0-0-1, 0.5-0-0.5) and Latin sigs (OD, BD, TDS, QID, HS, AC, PC).
• Levenshtein Typo Auto-Correction: Intelligent fuzzy matching fixes messy doctor handwriting and camera blur against a comprehensive 200+ clinical drug dictionary.
• Senior Audio Read-Out: One-tap voice narrator reads extracted medications, doses, and food timings aloud for low-vision patients.

⚠️ REAL-TIME DRUG INTERACTION (DDI) & FOOD SAFETY
• Instant Cross-Check: Cross-checks new prescriptions against your active medications and foods.
• Color-Coded Warnings:
  - Critical: Dual Beta-Blocker bradycardia, Anticoagulant + NSAID internal bleeding, Nitrate + PDE5 fatal hypotension.
  - Moderate: ACEI/ARB + Diuretic hyperkalemia, Antibiotic + Antacid chelation.
  - Advisory: Eye drop spacing protocols and food contraindications (e.g. Grapefruit + Statins).

🤖 OFFLINE ON-DEVICE AI HEALTH TUTOR & VOICE ASSISTANT
• 100% Private & Offline: Zero internet required. Your health data never leaves your device.
• 15+ Languages: Supports English, Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam, Bengali, Marathi, Gujarati, Punjabi, Spanish, Arabic, French, German, and Chinese.
• Voice In & Voice Out: Speak your medical questions and hear natural spoken answers.
• Patient Academy: Interactive masterclasses and quizzes explaining medical concepts in plain language.
• In-Chat Quick-Scheduling: Schedule medications straight from conversation with one tap.

📊 DOCTOR-READY PDF ADHERENCE REPORTS
• Formal Clinical Summary: Generate physician-ready PDF reports with medication names, formulations, Taken Count, Missed Count, and adherence percentages.
• Gamified Streaks & Heatmap: Track your consistency with monthly calendar heatmaps, streak counters, and motivational milestone badges.

🛡️ CAREGIVER SAFETY NET & APP LOCK
• Caregiver Escalation: Automatically alerts designated family or caregivers if critical doses are repeatedly skipped.
• Biometric & PIN Lock: Protect sensitive medical records with Fingerprint, Face ID, or a 4-digit PIN.

Download MediMind Pro today and take control of your medication safety!

Disclaimer: MediMind Pro provides medication organization and educational information. It is not a diagnostic medical device. Always consult your physician or pharmacist for medical decisions.
```

### Play Console Permissions Justification
- `android.permission.SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM`: Mandatory for medical-grade exact alarms (insulin, cardiovascular, glaucoma drops).
- `android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS`: Required for Android 13+ dose notifications.
- `android.permission.RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED`: Reschedules patient dose alarms after device reboot.
- `android.permission.CAMERA`: Strictly used for local on-device ML Kit prescription scanning. No photos are transmitted over the network.
- `android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO`: Strictly used for local speech-to-text voice questions.
- `android.permission.USE_BIOMETRIC`: Provides local biometric lock to safeguard personal medical information.

## 🚀 Step-by-Step Publishing Guide

### Generating Launcher Icons on Device
Run the following command in terminal to generate all launcher icons:
```powershell
flutter pub run flutter_launcher_icons
```

### Generating Android Release Bundle (AAB)
```powershell
flutter build appbundle --release
```
*Output: `build/app/outputs/bundle/release/app-release.aab`*

### Generating iOS Archive (IPA)
```bash
flutter build ipa --release
```
*Upload via Xcode Organizer or Transporter app to App Store Connect.*
